# 水蛭（Leech）[Anki, SuperMemo]

> [原文链接](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/307602868)

**Leeches **in [SuperMemo](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//supermemo.guru/wiki/SuperMemo) are questions that a student keeps [forgetting](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//supermemo.guru/wiki/Forgetting). Most often leeches come from disrespect for the [20 rules of knowledge formulation](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//supermemo.guru/wiki/20_rules_of_knowledge_formulation). Some well-formulated material may also need a boost from mnemonic techniques or a boost in [coherence](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//supermemo.guru/wiki/Coherence), e.g. by setting questions in relevant context.

SuperMemo 中的 Leech 指的是学生经常遗忘的问题。大多数情况下，Leech 来自对知识表述的 20 条规则[1]的不尊重。一些表述良好的材料可能还需要加强助记技巧或增强连贯性[2]，例如在相关的上下文设置问题。
